Calibrate

  1. Add monitor, then click the 4 corners of its screen: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left. You can drag the corners afterwards to fine-tune.
  2. Select the monitor. On its straightened screen, drag a box tightly around one number (digits only, no labels or units) and say what it is.
  3. Test reading to check every number, then re-read past pictures so earlier stills use the new settings.

Saves storage and keeps the stored evidence to the point. Turn it on only after every monitor on this camera is marked.
